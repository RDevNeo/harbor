Microsoft Visual C++ runtime (Windows build of Harbor)

harbor.exe for Windows carries three files of Microsoft's Visual C++ 2015-2022 Redistributable (x64),
version 14.44.35211: msvcp140.dll, vcruntime140.dll and vcruntime140_1.dll. Slint's Windows addon needs
them, and a new Windows doesn't have them. Harbor places them beside that addon when it starts, for its
own use only ("app-local").

terms of Microsoft's Visual Studio licence allow. They are not covered by Harbor's licence. They come from
Microsoft's own installer, VC_redist.x64.exe, checked by its SHA-256
