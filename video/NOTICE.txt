libmpv-harbor.so, the video player Harbor uses, is libmpv with FFmpeg and the libraries below linked
into one shared library. The library as a whole is under the GNU Lesser General Public License
version 2.1 or later: FFmpeg and mpv were built without their GPL parts (mpv: -Dgpl=false; FFmpeg:
neither --enable-gpl nor --enable-nonfree).

  mpv          LGPL 2.1 or later   https://mpv.io
  FFmpeg       LGPL 2.1 or later   https://ffmpeg.org
  libplacebo   LGPL 2.1 or later   https://libplacebo.org
  FriBidi      LGPL 2.1 or later   https://github.com/fribidi/fribidi
  libass       ISC                 https://github.com/libass/libass
  HarfBuzz     MIT                 https://harfbuzz.github.io
  FreeType     FTL                 https://freetype.org
  dav1d        BSD 2-Clause        https://code.videolan.org/videolan/dav1d
  zlib         zlib                https://zlib.net
  fast_float   MIT or Apache-2.0   https://github.com/fastfloat/fast_float
  PipeWire     MIT (headers only)  https://pipewire.org
  nv-codec-headers  MIT (headers)  https://github.com/FFmpeg/nv-codec-headers
  Vulkan-Headers  Apache-2.0 or MIT (headers, not used)  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/Vulkan-Headers

It finds the system's libva, EGL, PipeWire, PulseAudio and ALSA at run time when they're there; none
of them is part of it. Harbor itself (MIT) only loads this library at run time. The sources of all of it, the one small
change made to mpv, and the script that builds it are in harbor-video-source.tar.gz, published with
every Harbor release beside this file's archive. You may change them, build your own and put it in
place of libmpv-harbor.so in Harbor's install folder: Harbor checks the library it downloads, not
the one it loads, though its next update of this library replaces yours.
